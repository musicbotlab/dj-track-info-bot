# Now Playing AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Now Playing AI handles information when the Discord bot is used.

## Information processed

Depending on how the bot is configured, Now Playing AI may process:

- Discord server IDs
- Discord channel IDs
- Discord user IDs or usernames where needed for bot interactions
- Messages or commands sent directly to the bot
- Artist names
- Track titles
- Mix or version names
- Record label information
- Other music metadata supplied to the bot

The bot is not designed to collect sensitive personal information.

## How information is used

Information is processed only for purposes connected with operating the bot, including:

- identifying music being submitted to the service
- generating information about tracks and artists
- responding to Discord commands
- delivering generated responses to Discord
- troubleshooting, security, and service operation

## Artificial intelligence processing

Music metadata and related prompts may be sent to third-party artificial intelligence services in order to generate responses.

Only information required to perform the relevant request should be sent to these services.

## Discord

Now Playing AI operates through Discord. Discord separately processes information according to its own privacy policies and platform terms.

## Data retention

Information should only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for service operation, troubleshooting, security, or legal obligations.

Temporary processing data may be removed automatically after requests are completed.

## Data sharing

Personal information is not sold.

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the bot, including hosting providers, Discord, artificial intelligence providers, and related infrastructure services.

## Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information processed by the service.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your rights

Users may request information about personal data associated with their use of the bot, or request deletion where applicable.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the bot develops or its data-processing practices change.

## Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests can be sent to the operator of Now Playing AI using the contact details published alongside the application.
